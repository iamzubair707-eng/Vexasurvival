# Vexa Survival — Game Design

## Colour Theme
Primary: Dark Black + Red
Buttons: Green, Yellow, Orange
Premium items: Purple

## Reward Timing
- Daily login: 1 VEXA (streak bonus: 7 days = 10 VEXA)
- Every 15 min: 0.1 VEXA (claim button)
- Raid win: 2 VEXA

## Fear Mechanics
- Base can be raided while offline
- Shield available (24 hrs = 5 VEXA)
- Notification: "⚠️ Your base is under attack!"

## Social Features
- Clans: Max 50 members
- Global leaderboard (top 100)
- Chat system

## Urgency Elements
- Daily login reset at 00:00 UTC
- Limited time events (weekends)
- Timer on shields/speed-ups
